Deal Radar

Black Friday deals, checked by hand

43 deals from 7 stores, each with its sale price, its usual price, its discount, the time it was checked and where the price was read. Nothing here is a guess: every price was read from the store or a deals source that names the store on 27 November 2026.

How this board works. Prices were checked on 27 November 2026 (UTC) at the store or in a deals source that names the store; each card says where. The discount is computed from the two prices shown. A new low is the lowest price seen for that product; a strong deal is a deep cut worth acting on; a routine sale is a real discount that is not the best price around. Prices move during Black Friday week, so re-check the store page before you pay.

Deal Radar is an independent roundup. Store names are used only to say where a deal is sold.
